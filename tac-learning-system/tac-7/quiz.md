## Answers

### A1
**B) Target Zero-Touch Engineering**

### A2
**B) Plan → Build → Test → Review → Generate → Ship**
The complete ZTE workflow for shipping end-to-end.

### A3
**C) Composable agentic primitives**
The secret is composable primitives, not any single technique.

### A4
**B) Git Work Trees**
Worktrees enable isolated environments for parallel agent execution.

### A5
**B) When confidence hits 90% and review becomes a bottleneck**
Progress when human review becomes the constraint, not the safety net.

### A6
**B) Traditional SDLC approaches**
Composable primitives outperform rigid SDLC lifecycles.

### A7
**B) Gradual and progressive**
The transition isn't binary - move gradually toward out-loop.

### A8
**B) Docker or agent-based containerization frameworks**
Multiple isolation strategies work for agent parallelization.

### A9
**B) Constructing agent pipelines**
The ADW layer constructs and orchestrates agent pipelines.

### A10
**B) Time spent building the agentic layer**
Investment in the agentic layer pays exponential returns.

### A11
**False** - ZTE makes codebases that run themselves possible.

### A12
**True** - Composable primitives are more flexible than traditional SDLC.

### A13
**False** - The transition is gradual, not binary.

### A14
**True** - Review is especially important for agent-generated code.

### A15
**True** - Your domain knowledge makes out-loop systems superior.

### A16
**False** - Workflows won't work perfectly initially - that's acceptable.

### A17
**True** - Different organizations need different compositions.

### A18
1. **In-Loop** (interactive prompting)
2. **Out-Loop** (PITER system)
3. **Zero-Touch Engineering** (codebase ships itself)

### A19
**ships** - "Zero-Touch Engineering is where your codebase literally ships itself."

### A20
**primitives** - "The secret of tactical agentic coding: it's about composable agentic primitives."

### A21
Composable agentic primitives are reusable building blocks you can combine to solve any engineering problem class. They're "the secret" because:
- They're more flexible than rigid SDLC stages
- Different compositions work for different project types (web apps, CLI tools, notebooks)
- They can be mixed and matched
- Each organization develops unique compositions
- They outperform traditional approaches as you move away from rigid lifecycles

### A22
The three levels are:
1. **In-Loop**: Interactive prompting back and forth with your agent
2. **Out-Loop**: High-level prompts running through PITER systems autonomously
3. **Zero-Touch Engineering (ZTE)**: Codebase ships itself without human intervention

Progress from In-Loop to Out-Loop as you build confidence. Progress from Out-Loop to ZTE when confidence hits 90% and human review becomes a bottleneck rather than a safety net.

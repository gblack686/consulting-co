## Answers

### A1
**B) Prioritize Agentics**

### A2
**C) At least 50%**
At least half of your engineering time should go to the agentic layer.

### A3
**B) An imaginary ring around your codebase that thickens as you scale agentics**
The ring grows as you invest more in the agentic layer.

### A4
**B) ADW directory, Prompts, Plans**
These three components form the minimum viable agentic layer.

### A5
**B) The minimal script to start agentic coding**
The gateway script is your entry point into agentic coding.

### A6
**B) It calls an agent**
The gateway script is unique because it invokes an agent.

### A7
**B) DevOps, infrastructure, database, and the app**
These traditional concerns form the application layer.

### A8
**B) All other tactics compressed into one**
Tactic #8 is the meta-tactic that encompasses all others.

### A9
**B) Problem classes**
Focus on classes of problems, not individual one-offs.

### A10
**B) In .claude commands**
Prompts are stored in .claude commands in the minimum viable setup.

### A11
**True** - Tactic #8 is the compression of all other tactics.

### A12
**False** - The agentic layer is where irreplaceable engineers spend more time.

### A13
**True** - Problem classes scale; one-offs don't.

### A14
**False** - The gateway script is distinctly different - it calls an agent.

### A15
**True** - The agentic layer contains machines with your engineering judgment.

### A16
**False** - You should spend 50%+ on the agentic layer.

### A17
**agentic** - "The irreplaceable engineer operates on the agentic layer much more often."

### A18
**pieces** or **primitives** - "Focus on the pieces that make the whole..."

### A19
**Agentic Layer:** Contains ADWs, prompts, plans, and agentic primitives. This is where irreplaceable engineers spend 50%+ of their time. It's the layer that enables autonomous work.

**Application Layer:** Contains DevOps, infrastructure, database, and the raw application code. This is the traditional software engineering layer.

The irreplaceable engineer operates on the agentic layer much more often than the application layer.

### A20
The minimum viable agentic layer requires:
1. **AI developer workflow directory** - Where ADWs live
2. **Prompts** - Stored in .claude commands
3. **Plans** - Specifications and templates

This is the minimum set of information needed to start building an agentic layer.

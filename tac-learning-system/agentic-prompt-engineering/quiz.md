## Answers

### A1
**B) You, Your Team, Your Agents**
The trifecta you engineer for in the age of agents.

### A2
**B) A reusable ad hoc static prompt**
Level 1 is the simplest - a one-off that becomes reusable.

### A3
**B) A sequential workflow with task list**
Level 2 adds the crucial workflow section with sequential tasks.

### A4
**C) Three times**
Three times marks a pattern - hand it off to an agent.

### A5
**B) Agents updating agents through prompts updating prompts**
The highest level of self-modifying prompts.

### A6
**B) Level 3-4**
Levels 3-4 provide the 80/20 for most practical applications.

### A7
**B) The workflow section (sequential task list)**
The workflow section is "S-tier usefulness" - the most important.

### A8
**B) A prompt that kicks off other workflows**
Delegation prompts orchestrate other prompts.

### A9
**B) A prompt used to create another prompt in a specific pattern**
Meta-prompts create prompts following templates.

### A10
**C) The prompt**
The prompt is now the fundamental unit of engineering.

### A11
**True** - The prompt is the new fundamental unit.

### A12
**True** - Prompts become force multipliers.

### A13
**True** - ZTE is the North Star of agentic coding.

### A14
**False** - Winners build REUSABLE, battle-tested prompt libraries.

### A15
**True** - Level 5 is Higher-Order Prompts.

### A16
**True** - Three times marks a pattern.

### A17
**False** - It includes you, your team, AND your agents.

### A18
| Level | Name |
|-------|------|
| 1 | **B) High Level Prompt** |
| 2 | **E) Workflow Prompt** |
| 3 | **C) Control Flow** |
| 4 | **D) Delegation Prompt** |

### A19
**engineering** - "The prompt is now the fundamental unit of engineering."

### A20
**battle-tested** - "Winners are building libraries of reusable battle-tested agentic prompts."

### A21
The 7 Levels:
1. **High Level Prompt** - Reusable ad hoc static prompt (1-3 sections)
2. **Workflow Prompt** - Sequential workflow with task list (game changer)
3. **Control Flow** - Adds flow control for domain-specific agents
4. **Delegation Prompt** - Kicks off other workflows
5. **Higher Order Prompts** - Prompts passing prompts
6. **Template Meta Prompt** - Creates prompts in patterns
7. **Self-Improving Prompt** - Agents updating agents

For most applications, Levels 3-4 provide the 80/20.

### A22
The Stakeholder Trifecta is:
1. **You** - Your personal workflow
2. **Your Team** - Shared team processes
3. **Your Agents** - The agentic layer

It matters because in the age of agents, you're no longer just engineering for humans. You're also engineering for agents. This is the "agentic shift" most engineers haven't made yet.

# GB Automation Style Guide - Visual DNA Template

## Brand Overview

GB Automation is a professional AI automation consultancy with a warm, modern, minimalist aesthetic. The brand combines technical sophistication with approachability through careful color choices, clean typography, and glass morphism design elements.

---

## Visual Style

### Color Palette

**Primary Background Colors:**
- **Cream Background**: `#F3F1E7` - Warm, soft base that provides visual comfort
- **Cream Panel**: `#E6E4D9` - Slightly darker cream for layered depth

**Accent Colors:**
- **Terracotta**: `#D97757` - Warm, energetic accent for CTAs and highlights
- **Text Main**: `#191919` - Deep charcoal for primary content
- **Text Muted**: `#5C5C5C` - Medium gray for secondary information

**Color Usage Guidelines:**
- Backgrounds should use cream tones for warmth and approachability
- Terracotta accents sparingly for emphasis and visual interest
- Maintain high contrast for readability (dark text on light backgrounds)
- Avoid pure white or pure black - use the defined cream and charcoal tones

### Aesthetic & Design Language

**Core Design Principles:**
- **Minimalist**: Clean, uncluttered compositions with generous white space
- **Warm**: Organic, inviting feel through earth tones and soft edges
- **Professional**: Polished, refined execution without being cold or sterile
- **Modern**: Contemporary design patterns with glass morphism effects

**Glass Morphism Treatment:**
- Backdrop blur: 12px
- Subtle transparency for layered panels
- Soft shadows for depth (no harsh drop shadows)
- Creates a sense of depth and sophistication

**Visual Texture:**
- Smooth gradients (subtle, not dramatic)
- Soft edges and rounded corners
- Breathing room - don't crowd elements
- Balanced negative space

### Typography

**Font Families:**
- **Headlines/Display**: 'Newsreader' (serif) - Classical, authoritative, readable
- **Body/UI**: 'Inter' (sans-serif) - Clean, modern, highly legible

**Typographic Hierarchy:**
- Large serif headlines for impact
- Sans-serif for body copy and UI elements
- Consistent spacing and line height
- Clear size differentiation between levels

### Mood & Emotional Tone

**Desired Emotional Response:**
- **Professional**: Trustworthy, competent, expert
- **Calm**: Not overwhelming, peaceful, considered
- **Trustworthy**: Reliable, established, credible
- **Modern**: Forward-thinking, innovative, current
- **Approachable**: Warm, human, accessible (not cold or robotic)

**Avoid:**
- Overly technical or sterile aesthetics
- Harsh, aggressive visual language
- Cluttered or busy compositions
- Cold, impersonal corporate feel

## Character DNA (Robot Agents)

### Form & Structure

**Overall Design Philosophy:**
- **Humanoid proportions** but clearly mechanical (not attempting to look human)
- **Modular construction** with visible component separation
- **Industrial refinement** - technical but polished, not crude or rough
- **Functional aesthetic** - every element serves a purpose

**Key Characteristics:**
- Sleek metallic bodies with exposed mechanical details
- Visible gears, circuits, and internal mechanisms
- Articulated joints showing movement capability
- Distinct head/torso/limb structure

### Materials & Finish

**Primary Materials:**
- **Brushed metal**: Matte metallic finish, not glossy chrome
- **Polished chrome**: Accent areas for contrast
- **Copper/Bronze accents**: Warm metallic tones that complement terracotta brand color
- **Transparent panels**: Showing internal mechanisms

**Surface Treatment:**
- Mix of matte and reflective surfaces for visual interest
- Weathering or patina acceptable for character depth
- Technical details like screws, bolts, seams visible
- Clean but not pristine - shows they've been working

### Style & Personality Expression

**Character Through Design:**
- **Technical yet warm**: Industrial materials with organic poses
- **Intelligent appearance**: Thoughtful positioning, complex details
- **Approachable**: Not threatening or overly aggressive designs
- **Specialized roles**: Each robot has visual cues to their function

**Individual Character Traits:**
- **NEURAL-01 Generalist**: Balanced proportions, general-purpose tools, versatile stance
- **LOGIC-X9 Architect**: Precision tools, blueprint holders, analytical posture
- **ATLAS-M2 Analyst**: Data interfaces, screens, processing indicators
- **SENTRY-V4 Guardian**: Sturdy build, protective stance, alert posture
- **ECHO-D7 Interface**: Communication arrays, friendly gestures, open positioning

### Proportions & Scale

**Body Ratios:**
- Roughly humanoid 7-8 heads tall
- Limbs proportional but can be slightly exaggerated for function
- Head size allows for expressive design elements
- Torso contains visible core mechanisms

**Size Relationships:**
- Human-scale (not giant mechs or tiny drones)
- Can interact with standard tools and environments
- Presence without being overwhelming

### Technical Details

**Visible Components:**
- **Gears & Mechanisms**: Show how joints articulate
- **Circuits & Wiring**: Visible pathways, organized routing
- **Power Sources**: Glowing cores, energy indicators
- **Sensors & Optics**: Camera lenses, scanning arrays, LED indicators

**Construction Aesthetic:**
- Modular panels that could be opened/removed
- Fasteners and connection points visible
- Different material zones for different functions
- Clean cable management (organized, not chaotic)

## Lighting & Composition

### Lighting Style

**Primary Lighting:**
- **Soft, diffused illumination** - avoids harsh shadows
- **Warm color temperature** - matches cream/terracotta palette
- **Directional but gentle** - shows form without drama
- **Natural-feeling** - as if from large windows or soft studio lights

**Accent Lighting:**
- **Occasional dramatic highlights** for focal points
- **Rim lighting** to separate subjects from backgrounds
- **Glow effects** from robot power sources or displays
- **Motivated lighting** - light sources make sense in context

**Lighting Scenarios:**
- Golden hour quality for warmth
- Soft overcast for even illumination
- Studio lighting for product-style shots
- Environmental lighting that fits the scene

### Composition Guidelines

**Framing & Balance:**
- **Spacious compositions** - subjects have room to breathe
- **Rule of thirds** for dynamic but balanced layouts
- **Clean backgrounds** - minimal distractions
- **Focused depth** - clear subject hierarchy

**Camera Approach:**
- **Professional product photography style** for robots
- **Slight low angles** for aspirational feel (not extreme)
- **Eye-level or slightly above** for neutral, approachable shots
- **Environmental context** when showing robots in action

**Depth & Layering:**
- **Subtle depth of field** - keeps focus on subject
- **Foreground/midground/background** separation for dimension
- **Atmospheric perspective** - slight haze for depth in landscapes
- **Sharp focus** on primary subject, gentle falloff elsewhere

### Effects & Post-Processing

**Cinematic Quality:**
- Film-like color grading (not overly saturated)
- Slight vignette for focus direction (subtle)
- Clean highlights without blown-out whites
- Rich shadows with detail retention

**Visual Effects:**
- Subtle lens flares when appropriate
- Motion blur for dynamic action (sparingly)
- Particle effects (sparks, dust, light rays) for atmosphere
- Glow/bloom on light sources and displays

**Technical Execution:**
- High dynamic range (HDR) feel
- Crisp detail in focus areas
- Clean edges without over-sharpening
- Proper exposure across tonal range

## Application Guidelines

### For Character Variations
When generating new poses or scenes with existing characters:
1. Maintain the specific robot's material palette and proportions
2. Use consistent lighting approach (soft, warm)
3. Keep the character's personality traits visible
4. Ensure brand colors appear in environment or accents

### For New Concepts
When creating brand-consistent marketing assets or new concepts:
1. Start with cream/terracotta color palette
2. Apply glass morphism aesthetic if applicable
3. Use warm, professional lighting
4. Maintain minimalist, spacious composition
5. Include organic warmth despite technical subject matter

### For Image Series
When generating multiple related images:
1. Use consistent lighting direction across images
2. Maintain color palette harmony
3. Keep composition style uniform
4. Use same camera distance/angle approach
5. Ensure visual continuity in backgrounds and environments

### For Marketing Assets
When creating promotional or social media content:
1. Lead with terracotta accent for brand recognition
2. Use cream backgrounds for consistency with website
3. Show robots in relatable, professional contexts
4. Balance technical details with approachability
5. Maintain clean, uncluttered layouts

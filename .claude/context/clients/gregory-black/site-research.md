## Company Overview

Accenture Federal Services (AFS) is a wholly-owned subsidiary of Accenture LLP dedicated exclusively to serving the US federal government. The company brings together commercial innovation, advanced R&D, and deep federal expertise to help agencies accomplish mission-critical priorities.

**Tagline**: "Nothing matters more than helping the federal government do the extraordinary things it takes to make the nation stronger, safer -- and life better for everyone."

## 3. Avenoo.com

**Status:** Live, with functioning web app at app.avenoo.com
**Tech Stack:** WordPress (marketing site), custom web app (app.avenoo.com), iframe pricing from stage.avenoo.com
**Screenshot:** `avenoo-home.png`

### Tagline
> "RETAILERS AND VENDORS: LET'S GET ON THE SAME PAGE."
> "Product purchasing and vendor management is too f*****g chaotic."

### What Is Avenoo
Avenoo is a **wholesale vendor management platform** -- a SaaS tool that connects specialty retailers with product vendors. It compiles "the most fundamental aspects of product evaluation and vendor interaction into a practical toolkit."

### Value Proposition

**For Retailers:**
- Manage communication with new & existing vendors
- Discover qualified brand partners on their own terms
- Buy better, smarter, and faster
- Eliminate unsolicited vendor calls/emails/store visits
- Avoid losing touch with new brands they actually like

**For Vendors:**
- Communicate real-time with highly qualified potential retail partners
- Organize and send brand information to qualified retailers
- Get to the final purchasing decision faster
- Gain clarity in the buying process
- Maintain control over brand image within a single hub

### Proprietary System: Retail Access Code
Avenoo generates a **unique code for each retailer**. Retailers selectively share that code with vendors they want to work with, which validates the retailer's interest. This is the core mechanism that filters noise and ensures vendors only reach interested buyers.

### Key Features (from UI)
- **Brand Partners** -- view current brand partners and invite new ones
- **Certified Submissions** -- evaluate product submissions and promotions from potential vendors
- **Savings Calculator** -- interactive tool that calculates retail savings based on: inbound customer calls, store hours, staff hours spent with vendors, average ticket size, customer return rate, number of store locations

### Pricing Structure
- Three tiers: **Starter**, **Premium**, **Professional**
- Monthly and Annual billing options
- Separate pricing for Retailers and Vendors
- Pricing details loaded via iframe from `stage.avenoo.com` (specific dollar amounts not visible in snapshot)
- **Free one-month trial** available for Basic Plan

### CTAs
- Schedule a Demo
- Start a One-Month Free Trial
- "Play Helicopter" (gamification/fun element)
- App login: `app.avenoo.com`
- Unlock Retailer page: `avenoo.com/code/`

### Design Notes
- Purple-to-blue gradient color scheme
- Casual, irreverent copywriting tone ("too f*****g chaotic", "Launch Math Robots", "you just hate reading")
- Product screenshots/mockups embedded throughout
- Modern SaaS marketing site layout

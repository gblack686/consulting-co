## Brand Overview

Tequila Cayeya (stylized "Cayeya") is the flagship brand of Piermont Brands, Inc., a spirits holding company founded in 2019 by Charles Sachs and Sam Hirsch. The brand produces single-barrel, small-batch tequila from 100% Blue Weber agave grown on a single estate in the lowland (Los Valles) region of Jalisco, Mexico. Production is contract-distilled at Destiladora de Agave Azul under NOM 1424, with some expressions also linked to NOM 1414 (Feliciano Vivanco y Asociados).

The brand is guided by Master Destiladora Zandra Gomez, who oversees production from agave selection through distillation. Cayeya differentiates through single-barrel releases, sustainable packaging (recycled Coca-Cola bottles), and a strict allocation-based release schedule.

**Tagline:** "Take care of the land and the land will take care of you"
**Positioning:** Ultra-premium, craft, sustainable, limited-production tequila
**Key Differentiators:**
- Single-barrel production (not blended across barrels)
- Female Master Distiller (Zandra Gomez)
- 100% recycled packaging (bottles from recycled Coca-Cola glass, recycled corks/labels/hang tags)
- Single-estate agave aged 6-9 years
- Additive-free
- Allocation-based release schedule creating scarcity

### Tasting Profile Summary

**Blanco:** Agave-forward, citrus (lime), stone fruit (plum, apricot), pistachio, white pepper finish. Full-bodied mouthfeel. Review score: 90/100 Silver Medal (Best Tasting Spirits).

**Reposado:** Vanilla, caramel, spice, floral undertones from dual-cask aging.

**Anejo:** Rich agave, oak, coffee, toffee, caramelized agave, dark chocolate, espresso on the finish.

**Cristalino:** Cooked agave, sweet caramel, toasted oak, cinnamon, baking spices. Crystal clear despite anejo aging.

### Production Specifications

- **Agave:** 100% Blue Weber, single estate, lowland (Los Valles), 6-9 year maturity
- **Water:** Deep well water
- **Cooking:** Masonry brick ovens
- **Crushing:** Classic roller mill
- **Fermentation:** Stainless steel tanks, without fibers, less oxygen exposure for single barrels
- **Distillation:** Small batch, double distillation
- **NOM:** 1424 (primary), 1414 (some expressions)
- **Distillery:** Destiladora de Agave Azul S.A. de C.V.

## Brand Story & Positioning

### Origin Story
Charles Sachs and Sam Hirsch founded Piermont Brands in 2019 with Cayeya as their debut brand. The brand name "Cayeya" draws on Mexican heritage and the concept of taking care of the land as a reciprocal relationship. The founders partnered with Master Destiladora Zandra Gomez, emphasizing the rarity of a female-led tequila production.

### Positioning
Cayeya occupies the **ultra-premium craft tequila** segment, competing with brands like Clase Azul, Fortaleza, Tears of Llorona, and G4 rather than mass-market brands. Key positioning pillars:

1. **Scarcity / Exclusivity:** Single-barrel production, strict release schedules, 1,200-bottle limited editions
2. **Sustainability:** Fully recycled packaging, single-estate sourcing
3. **Craft Authenticity:** Traditional brick-oven cooking, roller mill, female master distiller
4. **Collector / Gifting Appeal:** Magnetic gift boxes, suede accents, pewter labels, glass toppers on limited editions

### Barrel Program (B2B / Private Client)
Available to restaurants, select retailers, and private clients in limited quantities:
- **Reposado releases:** 3x/year (February, June, November)
- **Anejo releases:** 2x/year (April, September)
- **Experimental Series:** 1x/year (October)
- Allocation-based, dependent on market size and distillery inventory.
- Inquiry via form or email to info@tequilacayeya.com.

## Brand Voice & Tone

### Voice Characteristics
- **Luxury-aspirational:** "distinction," "exclusivity," "uncompromising quality," "artesanal"
- **Heritage-rooted:** "time-honored techniques," "forged through," "tradition"
- **Sustainability-forward:** "reborn from recycled," "take care of the land"
- **Scarcity-driven:** "highly limited," "select community," "strict release schedule"
- **Sensory-rich:** Detailed tasting notes with multi-layered descriptions

### Messaging Themes
1. **Single-barrel exclusivity** — Every expression is unique, numbered, signed by the master distiller
2. **Sustainability as luxury** — Recycled packaging positioned as premium craft, not compromise
3. **Female master distiller** — Zandra Gomez highlighted as differentiator in male-dominated industry
4. **Collectibility** — Limited editions with premium packaging (suede, pewter, magnetic boxes)
5. **Community / insider access** — "Cayeya Club," barrel program, allocation model

### Content on Site
- **Blog:** Limited — cocktail recipes only (Margarita, Paloma, Tequila Sour)
- **Brand pages:** Experience page (brand philosophy), Barrel Program page
- **Product descriptions:** Detailed, sensory-rich, emphasizing production process

## Automation Opportunity Areas

### High-Impact Opportunities

1. **Social Media Expansion & Automation**
   - No TikTok, Facebook, Twitter/X, or YouTube presence despite having tracking pixels installed
   - Instagram-only strategy with only 82 posts and ~4,700 followers is severely underweight for a premium spirits brand
   - Automated content scheduling, cross-posting, and social listening

2. **Email/SMS Marketing Automation (Klaviyo Optimization)**
   - Cayeya Club newsletter exists but unclear automation sophistication
   - Barrel program release notifications (3 Reposado, 2 Anejo, 1 Experimental per year) — perfect for drip campaigns
   - Post-purchase flows, abandoned cart recovery, VIP segmentation
   - Release countdown sequences for limited editions

3. **Store Locator / Distribution Visibility**
   - Current store locator page is non-functional (empty/placeholder)
   - Needs dynamic locator integration (e.g., Destini, Where to Buy, or custom solution)
   - Could feed retail partner data into CRM for B2B relationship tracking

4. **CRM / Customer Data Platform**
   - No visible CRM beyond Klaviyo
   - Barrel program inquiries via email/form — needs structured pipeline
   - Private client / restaurant relationships need tracking
   - Celebrity collaboration tracking (Dave East, future partners)

5. **Content & SEO**
   - Blog has only 3 cocktail recipes — massive content gap
   - No brand storytelling content, production videos, distillery content, or educational content
   - Agave education, cocktail culture, sustainability stories are all high-value SEO topics

6. **Loyalty / Subscription Program**
   - No loyalty program exists
   - Allocation-based model is ripe for tiered loyalty (early access, exclusive releases)
   - Subscription box or tasting club potential

7. **B2B Barrel Program Digitization**
   - Currently managed via email/form submissions
   - Could be systematized: application portal, allocation tracking, barrel selection dashboard
   - Release calendar automation with waitlist management

8. **Influencer & Partnership Management**
   - Dave East collaboration exists but no visible system for tracking ROI
   - Tequila influencer space is active (30+ major tequila influencers identified)
   - Need influencer CRM, gifting tracking, content rights management

9. **Customer Support / Chatbot**
   - No visible helpdesk or chat on site
   - FAQ page is minimal (4 questions)
   - AI chatbot for product recommendations, cocktail recipes, availability questions

10. **Analytics & Attribution**
    - GA4 + Facebook Pixel + TikTok Pixel + Clarity installed but unclear if unified
    - No visible dashboard or attribution model across channels
    - DTC vs. retail sales tracking opportunity

## Company Overview

**Legal Name:** Piermont Brands, Inc.
**Also operates as:** Chica Chida LLC (for the Chica~Chida brand)
**Founded:** 2019
**Headquarters:** 385 Sylvan Avenue, Suite 14, Englewood Cliffs, NJ 07632
**Phone:** 201-681-8001
**Type:** Privately held wine & spirits supplier / importer
**Funding Raised:** ~$880K (per CB Insights)
**Employee Count:** Small team (~5-10 estimated based on LinkedIn profiles)
**Trademarks Owned:** 8 registered trademarks

Piermont Brands is a dynamic and rapidly growing wine and spirits supplier headquartered in northern New Jersey. The company positions itself as a challenger brand in the beverage alcohol industry, emphasizing craft production, sustainability, and unconventional marketing. Their stated mission is "reshaping the beverage alcohol industry by delivering spirits that defy convention."

## Brand Portfolio

### 1. Tequila Cayeya (Flagship)
- **Website:** tequilacayeya.com (Shopify)
- **NOM:** 1424 (Destiladora de Agave Azul, Jalisco)
- **Production:** Single-barrel, small-batch tequila. 100% agave grown on a single estate for 6-9 years in lowland region. Deep well water.
- **Master Distiller:** Zandra Gomez
- **Sustainability:** Bottles hand-crafted from recycled Coca-Cola glass at a family-owned artisanal glass factory in Jalisco. Corks, labels, hang tags all recycled materials.
- **Product Line & Pricing:**
  - Blanco -- $49.99
  - Blended Reposado -- $55.00
  - Single Barrel Reposado (11.5 months: 9 months re-charred American oak + 2.5 months French oak) -- $129.99
  - Single Barrel Anejo (15.5 months in re-charred American oak, cask strength finish) -- $159.99
  - Angel's Limited Edition Anejo -- $150.00
  - Diablo Cristalino (sold out) -- $150.00
- **Barrel Program:** Available for custom barrel selections
- **Distillery Plans:** Exploring construction of their own distillery in Mexico
- **Instagram:** @tequilacayeya (~4,739 followers, 85 posts)
- **Email:** info@tequilacayeya.com
- **Newsletter:** "Cayeya Club"
- **Payment Methods:** Visa, MC, Amex, Discover, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal, Shop Pay

## AI / Automation Opportunity Areas

Based on the company profile, tech stack, and Michael Fisch's mention of Airtable + N8n, here are the highest-value automation opportunities:

### 1. Airtable + N8n Workflow Optimization
- **Current state:** Likely using Airtable as a lightweight database for operations
- **Opportunity:** Build robust N8n workflows connecting Airtable to Shopify, email, distributor portals, and financial systems
- **Use cases:** Auto-sync Shopify orders to Airtable, inventory alerts, distributor order tracking, SKU management across 3+ brands

### 2. Multi-Brand E-Commerce Unification
- **Problem:** Three separate websites on three different platforms (Shopify, Webflow, Wix) with no unified backend
- **Opportunity:** Centralized order management, unified customer data, cross-brand analytics dashboard
- **Approach:** N8n as the integration layer connecting all three storefronts to a single Airtable or CRM backend

### 3. Distributor & Sales Operations
- **Problem:** Managing relationships with 5+ major distributors (SGWS, Republic, Breakthru, Allied, Fedway) across expanding markets
- **Opportunity:** Automated depletion reporting, reorder alerts, market expansion tracking
- **Tools:** Airtable for distributor CRM + N8n for automated reporting and notifications

### 4. Financial Reporting Automation (Michael Fisch's domain)
- **Problem:** Fractional CFO needs efficient financial visibility across multiple brands/entities
- **Opportunity:** Auto-pull Shopify/Webflow revenue data, connect to QuickBooks, generate automated P&L by brand
- **Tools:** N8n connecting Shopify API + Webflow API -> QuickBooks/Xero + Airtable dashboard

### 5. Social Media & Influencer ROI Tracking
- **Problem:** Heavy investment in influencer marketing (Caleb Pressley) but likely no automated ROI tracking
- **Opportunity:** Auto-track social mentions, engagement metrics, correlate with sales spikes
- **Tools:** N8n with Instagram API, TikTok API, Shopify sales data -> Airtable analytics base

### 6. Compliance & Regulatory
- **Problem:** Alcohol industry has complex state-by-state compliance requirements
- **Opportunity:** Automated compliance tracking, license renewal reminders, state reporting
- **Approach:** Airtable compliance database + N8n scheduled alerts

### 7. Customer Experience / DTC
- **Problem:** Two DTC storefronts with separate customer lists
- **Opportunity:** Unified email marketing, cross-brand upsell automation, loyalty program
- **Tools:** N8n connecting Shopify + Webflow customer data -> unified email platform

description: Show overall statistics from the knowledge graph
---

Show comprehensive statistics from the Neo4j/Graphiti knowledge graph.

Query Neo4j to gather:

1. **Overall Metrics**:
   - Total episodes in the graph
   - Total entities extracted
   - Total relationships
   - Date range (first to last episode)

2. **Knowledge Density**:
   - Average entities per episode
   - Most productive day (most episodes)
   - Most knowledge-dense day (highest entity count)

3. **Top Concepts** (Top 10):
   - Most connected entities with connection counts
   - Most frequently mentioned entities

4. **Project Breakdown**:
   - Episodes per project (from episode names)
   - Active projects

5. **Recent Activity**:
   - Episodes in last 24 hours
   - Episodes in last 7 days
   - Entities created in last 7 days

6. **Growth Trends**:
   - Daily episode creation rate
   - Knowledge graph growth over time

Display as a beautiful formatted report with insights and patterns you notice.

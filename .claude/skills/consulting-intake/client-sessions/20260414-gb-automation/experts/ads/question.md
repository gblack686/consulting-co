# Ads Questions

## Campaign Strategy Questions

### Before Launching
1. What's the primary goal? (leads, traffic, awareness)
2. Who exactly are we targeting?
3. What's the offer/CTA?
4. What's the budget and timeline?
5. What does success look like?

### Audience Definition
6. What job titles make decisions?
7. What company sizes are ideal?
8. What industries respond best?
9. What pain points resonate most?
10. Where are they in the buyer journey?

### Offer Strategy
11. What's valuable enough to exchange for contact info?
12. Is the offer aligned with our service?
13. How quickly can we follow up?
14. What's the natural next step after the offer?

## Campaign Optimization Questions

### Performance Analysis
15. What's the CPL trend over time?
16. Which audience segments perform best?
17. Which creatives have best CTR?
18. Where are leads dropping off?
19. What's the lead-to-call conversion?

### Diagnosis Questions
20. If CPL is high - is it targeting, creative, or offer?
21. If CTR is low - is the hook working?
22. If conversions are low - is the landing page aligned?
23. If lead quality is poor - is targeting too broad?

### Testing Questions
24. What's the one thing to test next?
25. Do we have enough data to make a decision?
26. What would we do differently if starting over?
27. Is this worth scaling or should we try something new?

## Lead Quality Questions

### After Lead Comes In
28. Does this lead match our ICP?
29. Did they engage with the content?
30. How quickly did we follow up?
31. Did they respond to outreach?
32. Did they book a call?

### Quality Indicators
33. What lead source produces best calls?
34. What offer produces best fit?
35. What targeting produces decision-makers?
36. Are we attracting buyers or browsers?

## Budget Questions

### Allocation
37. Are we spending in the right places?
38. What's the opportunity cost of this spend?
39. Should we shift budget between campaigns?
40. When should we scale/cut?

### ROI Calculation
41. What's our true cost per qualified lead?
42. What's our cost per booked call?
43. What's our cost per closed deal?
44. What would 2x budget get us?

## Creative Questions

### Before Creating
45. What's the single most important message?
46. What proof can we show?
47. What will stop them scrolling?
48. What's the clearest CTA?

### After Running
49. Which hook performed best?
50. Which proof point resonated?
51. What creative fatigue are we seeing?
52. What should we test next?

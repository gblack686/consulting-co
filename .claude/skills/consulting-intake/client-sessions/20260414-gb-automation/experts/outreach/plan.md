# Outreach Planning

## Daily Outreach Plan

### Morning Block (8-10 AM)
1. Review overnight responses (15 min)
2. Prioritize hot leads for immediate follow-up (10 min)
3. Send batch of connection requests (30 min)
4. Start new DM conversations (30 min)

### Afternoon Block (2-4 PM)
1. Follow up on pending conversations (30 min)
2. Research new prospects (30 min)
3. Personalize tomorrow's outreach batch (30 min)
4. Update CRM/Memory with all interactions (15 min)

## Weekly Outreach Plan

| Day | Focus | Target |
|-----|-------|--------|
| Monday | Fresh outreach, new week energy | 30 connections, 10 DMs |
| Tuesday | Follow-ups + new outreach | 25 connections, 15 DMs |
| Wednesday | Mid-week push, book calls | 25 connections, 15 DMs |
| Thursday | Heavy follow-up day | 20 connections, 20 DMs |
| Friday | Nurture + weekend prep | 15 connections, 10 DMs |
| Weekend | Research & list building | 0 outreach, 50 prospects researched |

## Prospect Prioritization Matrix

| Priority | Criteria | Action |
|----------|----------|--------|
| P1 - Hot | Engaged with our content, posted about pain points, mutual connections | Same-day personalized outreach |
| P2 - Warm | Fits ICP, active on LinkedIn, relevant industry | Next-day outreach |
| P3 - Cool | Fits ICP loosely, less active | Batch outreach |
| P4 - Cold | Aspirational, not ideal fit | Skip or low-effort |

## Planning Checklist

Before any outreach session:
- [ ] Prospect list prepared and prioritized
- [ ] Templates reviewed and personalized
- [ ] LinkedIn session warmed up (browse, engage)
- [ ] Calendar links working
- [ ] Response templates ready
- [ ] CRM/Memory open for logging

After any outreach session:
- [ ] All interactions logged
- [ ] Hot leads flagged
- [ ] Tomorrow's batch prepped
- [ ] Metrics updated
- [ ] Learnings noted

# Outreach Expertise

## Part 1: Domain Fundamentals

### What is B2B LinkedIn Outreach?
Strategic connection and conversation building on LinkedIn to generate qualified leads for consulting services. The goal is to start genuine conversations that lead to discovery calls, not to spam pitch.

### Core Principles
1. **Personalization over volume** - 20 highly personalized messages beat 100 generic ones
2. **Value before ask** - Lead with insights, offer help, earn the right to pitch
3. **Pattern interrupts** - Stand out from the flood of "I noticed your profile" messages
4. **Timing matters** - Tuesday-Thursday, 8-10 AM in prospect's timezone
5. **Follow-up is everything** - 80% of deals close after 5+ touches

## Part 2: Target Audience

### Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
| Attribute | Criteria |
|-----------|----------|

| Company Size | 1-50 employees |
| Industry | Consulting, Agency, Professional Services |

### Qualifying Signals
- Recently raised funding (capacity to spend)
- Posting about operational challenges
- Hiring for ops/admin roles (could automate instead)
- Engaged in AI/automation conversations
- Using tools we integrate with (Slack, Linear, etc.)

## Part 3: Outreach Frameworks

### The RABT Framework
1. **R**elevance - Why them specifically?
2. **A**uthority - Establish credibility fast
3. **B**enefit - What's in it for them?
4. **T**ransition - Clear next step

### Connection Request Templates

**Template A: Mutual Interest**
```
Hey {first_name} - saw your post about {topic}. Building something similar for {industry} folks. Would love to connect and swap notes.
```

**Template B: Specific Observation**
```
{first_name} - noticed you're scaling {company}. Been helping consultants automate their ops with AI. Thought there might be some overlap. Connect?
```

**Template C: Content Hook**
```
{first_name} - your take on {topic} resonated. Working on AI tools for exactly this problem. Would value your perspective.
```

### DM Sequence Framework

**Message 1 (after acceptance):**
```
Thanks for connecting, {first_name}! Quick q - what's the biggest time sink in your week that you wish you could automate?
```

**Message 2 (if response, 1 day later):**
```
That's exactly what I hear from most {role}s. We've been building AI assistants that handle {pain_point}. Want me to share how {similar_company} cut that down by 80%?
```

**Message 3 (if no response, 3 days later):**
```
{first_name} - just shipped a case study on automating {relevant_task} for {industry}. Thought you might find it useful: {link}
```

**Message 4 (if engaged, book the call):**
```
Sounds like there's real potential here. Want to do a quick 15-min call to see if our approach fits your setup? Here's my calendar: {link}
```

## Part 4: Tools & Automation

### LinkedIn Limits (Safety)
| Action | Daily Limit | Our Target |
|--------|-------------|------------|
| Connection requests | 100 | 30 |
| Messages | 150 | 50 |
| Profile views | 500 | 100 |
| InMail (if premium) | 50 | 0 |

### Workflow Automation
1. **Prospect list building** - Sales Navigator search, export to CSV
2. **Enrichment** - Add company info, recent posts, mutual connections
3. **Personalization** - Generate custom first line per prospect
4. **Queue management** - Stage messages for batch approval
5. **Response tracking** - Log all interactions in Memory

## Part 5: Metrics & Optimization

### Funnel Metrics
```
Prospects Identified
    └─> Connection Requests Sent (target: 30/day)
        └─> Connections Accepted (target: >30%)
            └─> DM Conversations Started (target: 100%)
                └─> Responses Received (target: >15%)
                    └─> Calls Booked (target: >20% of responses)
                        └─> Proposals Sent (target: >50% of calls)
```

### A/B Testing Protocol
1. Test ONE variable at a time
2. Minimum 50 sends per variant
3. Track: acceptance rate, response rate, call booking rate
4. Winner becomes new baseline
5. Log all tests in self-improve learnings

## Part 7: Patterns & Learnings

### What Works
- Mentioning specific content they posted
- Referencing mutual connections
- Short, casual tone (not corporate)
- Questions that show you did research
- Following up consistently (3-5 touches)

### What Doesn't Work
- Generic "I noticed your profile" openers
- Long pitches in first message
- Asking for calls before building rapport
- Sending connection requests without notes
- Giving up after one unanswered message

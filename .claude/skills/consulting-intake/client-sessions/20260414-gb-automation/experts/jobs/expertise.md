# Jobs Expertise

## Part 1: Domain Fundamentals

### What is Strategic Job Search for Consultants?
Using job boards and opportunities as a lead generation channel for consulting services. The goal isn't necessarily employment - it's finding companies with pain points we can solve, whether as a contractor, consultant, or project partner.

### Core Principles
1. **Quality over quantity** - 10 targeted applications beat 100 spray-and-pray
2. **Consulting angle** - Position as consultant/contractor, not employee
3. **Speed matters** - Apply within 24 hours of posting
4. **Personalization wins** - Tailor every application
5. **Network in** - Use LinkedIn to find internal connections

## Part 3: Application Strategy

### The Consultant Pitch
Frame every application as:
"I can help you solve {problem} in {timeframe}. Here's how I've done it before."

Not:
"I'm looking for a job and have relevant experience."

### Application Components

1. **Hook** (first line)
   - Reference something specific about their company
   - Mention a relevant result you've achieved
   
2. **Relevance** (1-2 lines)
   - Why you're specifically suited
   - Concrete skills/experience match

3. **Proof** (2-3 lines)
   - Similar project or result
   - Quantified outcome
   
4. **Offer** (1-2 lines)
   - What you can do for them
   - Specific suggestion

5. **CTA** (1 line)
   - Clear next step
   - Easy to say yes

### Cover Letter Template
```
{Hook referencing their specific need}

I've spent the last {X} years helping {similar companies} {achieve outcome}. Most recently, I helped {example company} {specific result}.

What I noticed about your {role/company}: {observation that shows you did homework}.

I'd love to explore how I could help {company} {achieve their goal}. Would a 15-minute call this week work?

Best,
Greg Black
GB Automation
{link to portfolio/case study}
```

## Part 4: Resume/Profile Strategy

### Consultant-Focused Resume
Focus on:
- Results achieved for clients
- Specific technologies/tools
- Project scope and outcomes
- Client types/industries

Not:
- Job titles at companies
- Responsibilities
- Soft skills without proof

### LinkedIn Optimization
- Headline: "AI Automation Consultant | Helping {ICP} save {outcome}"
- About: Problem-agitate-solution format
- Experience: Client results, not job duties
- Featured: Case studies, testimonials

## Part 5: Response Handling

### Response Types

| Response | Meaning | Action |
|----------|---------|--------|
| "We'd like to chat" | Interest | Schedule ASAP |
| "Send more info" | Lukewarm | Send case study + ask for call |
| "Not a fit right now" | Timing | Ask to stay in touch |
| "We went another direction" | Closed | Ask for feedback |
| No response (2 weeks) | Cold | Mark as dead |

### Interview Prep
For every call, prepare:
1. Their specific situation (research)
2. 3 questions about their needs
3. Relevant case study
4. Clear value proposition
5. Rate/terms ready

### Negotiation
- Start with project/consulting framing
- Quote monthly retainer, not hourly
- $3,500/month minimum
- Be willing to walk away
- Offer pilot if they're hesitant

## Part 7: Patterns & Learnings

### What Gets Responses
- Specific reference to their posting/company
- Concrete results with numbers
- Clear consulting angle (not "hire me")
- Short cover letters (<150 words)
- Applied within 24 hours

### What Gets Ignored
- Generic applications
- Long resumes
- No personalization
- Late applications (>1 week)
- Employee mindset

### Red Flags in Postings
- "Equity only" compensation
- Unrealistic requirements
- Vague about pay
- "Fast-paced" (often chaotic)
- 100+ applicants already

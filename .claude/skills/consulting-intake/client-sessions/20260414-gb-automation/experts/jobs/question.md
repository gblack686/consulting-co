# Jobs Questions

## Pre-Application Research Questions

1. What does this company actually do?
2. Who's the hiring manager?
3. What specific problem does this role solve?
4. What's their tech stack?
5. Are they VC-backed? Recent funding?
6. Any mutual connections I can leverage?
7. What have they posted about recently (LinkedIn, blog)?

## Interview Questions to Ask Them

### Understanding the Need
1. "What's driving the need for this role right now?"
2. "What would success look like in the first 90 days?"
3. "What's been tried before that didn't work?"
4. "Who would I be working with most closely?"
5. "What's the biggest challenge you're facing in this area?"

### Understanding Fit
6. "Is this a project or ongoing engagement?"
7. "What's your ideal timeline?"
8. "Are you open to contractor/consultant vs. full-time?"
9. "What's the decision-making process from here?"
10. "What's the budget range for this role?"

### Building Rapport
11. "How did you end up at {company}?"
12. "What's the most exciting thing happening at {company} right now?"
13. "What would you want the person in this role to accomplish that would make your life easier?"

## Questions They'll Ask (Prep Answers)

### Background
- "Tell me about yourself" → 60-second pitch focused on relevant results
- "Why are you interested in this role?" → Connect to their specific need
- "What's your experience with {technology}?" → Specific examples

### Consulting Framing
- "Are you open to full-time?" → "I work best as a consultant where I can focus on specific outcomes. I've found this delivers better results for both sides."
- "Why consulting vs. employment?" → "I can bring focused expertise without the overhead, and you get flexibility."

### Results
- "Tell me about a successful project" → STAR format with metrics
- "How do you handle {challenge}?" → Specific example
- "What would you do in your first 30 days?" → Ask clarifying questions, then outline approach

### Rate Discussion
- "What are your rate expectations?" → "For consulting engagements like this, I typically work on a monthly retainer of $X. What's the budget you're working with?"
- "That's higher than we expected" → "I understand. Let's discuss the scope - there might be a way to structure this that works for both of us."

## Application Refinement Questions

Before submitting:
1. Does my first line reference something specific about them?
2. Did I include a quantified result?
3. Is this under 150 words?
4. Is my CTA clear and easy?
5. Did I position as consultant, not employee?
6. Did I apply within 24 hours of posting?

## Post-Response Questions

After getting a response:
1. What do they seem most interested in?
2. What concerns might they have?
3. What case study is most relevant?
4. What's my rate strategy for this one?
5. Who else might be involved in the decision?

## Self-Reflection Questions

Weekly review:
1. Which applications got responses? Why?
2. Which got ignored? Why?
3. Am I targeting the right opportunities?
4. Is my messaging working?
5. What should I change next week?

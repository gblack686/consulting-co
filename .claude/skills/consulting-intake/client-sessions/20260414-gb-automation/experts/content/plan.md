# Content Planning

## Weekly Content Plan

### Sunday (Prep Day)
1. Review previous week's performance (30 min)
2. Identify 5 content topics for the week (30 min)
3. Draft Monday-Wednesday posts (1 hour)
4. Queue scheduled posts (15 min)

### Daily (Execution)
- 8 AM: Engage with 10 posts in feed
- 9 AM: Publish scheduled post
- 12 PM: Check and respond to comments
- 3 PM: Engage with 5 more posts
- 6 PM: Final comment responses

### Friday (Review)
- Analyze week's metrics
- Note top performer and why
- Capture ideas for next week
- Update self-improve learnings

## Content Batching

### Batch Session (2 hours)
1. **10 min**: Review content bank and ideas
2. **20 min**: Outline 5 posts
3. **60 min**: Write 5 drafts
4. **20 min**: Add visuals/formatting
5. **10 min**: Schedule all posts

### Content Bank Maintenance
Keep running list of:
- Client quotes and wins
- Questions received in DMs
- Comments that sparked ideas
- Competitor content to riff on
- Industry news to react to

## Planning Checklist

### Before Each Post
- [ ] Hook is in first 2 lines
- [ ] One clear main idea
- [ ] Specific details/numbers included
- [ ] CTA matches content goal
- [ ] Proofread for typos
- [ ] Visual (if needed) attached

### Weekly Review
- [ ] Posted 5+ times
- [ ] Engagement goal met
- [ ] Top performer identified
- [ ] Ideas captured for next week
- [ ] Self-improve cycle run

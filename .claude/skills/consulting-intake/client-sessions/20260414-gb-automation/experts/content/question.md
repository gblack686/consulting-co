# Content Questions

## Content Ideation Questions

### Finding Topics
1. "What's a question I got asked this week that others probably have too?"
2. "What's something I believe that most in my industry disagree with?"
3. "What mistake did I make recently that others can learn from?"
4. "What's working really well right now that I should share?"
5. "What did I learn from a client conversation today?"

### Deepening Topics
6. "What's the obvious advice everyone gives that's actually wrong?"
7. "What's a common misconception about [topic]?"
8. "If I had to explain this to a complete beginner, how would I?"
9. "What's the non-obvious second-order effect of this?"
10. "What would happen if you 10x'd this approach?"

### Client Story Mining
11. "What was the biggest transformation a client experienced?"
12. "What was the 'aha moment' in their journey?"
13. "What did they try before that didn't work?"
14. "What surprised them most about the results?"
15. "What would they tell someone considering the same thing?"

## Content Refinement Questions

### Before Publishing
- Does this hook stop the scroll?
- Is the main point clear in under 30 seconds?
- Is there a specific, actionable takeaway?
- Would I engage with this if someone else posted it?
- Does this sound like me or like corporate speak?

### After Publishing
- What type of comments did it generate?
- Did it attract the right audience?
- What questions did people ask?
- What follow-up content could this inspire?
- Would I post this again or iterate?

## Case Study Questions

### Discovery (for client)
1. "What was your situation before we worked together?"
2. "What had you tried that didn't work?"
3. "What made you decide to try something new?"
4. "What was the biggest concern you had going in?"
5. "Walk me through a typical day/week now vs. before."

### Results (for client)
6. "What specific metrics changed?"
7. "What surprised you most about the results?"
8. "What would you tell someone considering this?"
9. "Can we use your company name or keep it anonymous?"
10. "Would you be open to a short quote or video testimonial?"

## Lead Magnet Questions

### Topic Selection
- What's the #1 question prospects ask before buying?
- What's a quick win I could help them achieve?
- What tool/template do I use that others would find valuable?
- What's a common mistake I could help them avoid?

### Format Selection
- Is this better as a checklist, template, or guide?
- How much time should it take to consume?
- What's the ideal next step after they use this?
- Does this naturally lead to our service?

## Engagement Questions

### For Comments
- "Great point - what made you start thinking about this?"
- "Interesting take - have you tried [related approach]?"
- "Thanks for sharing - what's been the biggest challenge with that?"
- "Appreciate the perspective - what would you do differently?"

### For Conversations
- "What prompted you to reach out about this?"
- "What would success look like for you?"
- "What's the timeline you're working with?"
- "What's the biggest barrier right now?"

# Content Expertise

## Part 1: Domain Fundamentals

### What is B2B LinkedIn Content?
Strategic content creation that establishes expertise, builds trust, and generates inbound interest from target customers. The goal is to make prospects come to you by demonstrating you understand their problems and have solutions.

### Core Principles
1. **Teach, don't pitch** - Share genuine insights, let expertise sell itself
2. **Specificity wins** - "I helped a 3-person agency save 12 hours/week" beats "AI improves efficiency"
3. **Consistency compounds** - Daily posts build more than sporadic viral attempts
4. **Stories beat statistics** - One good client story outperforms ten data points
5. **Controversy (carefully) works** - Take positions, but don't be inflammatory

## Part 2: Content Strategy

1. **Behind the Build** (40%)

## Part 3: Post Frameworks

### The Hook-Story-CTA Framework
```
[HOOK - Pattern interrupt, bold claim, or question]

[STORY - 3-5 lines of context/journey]

[INSIGHT - What you learned]

[CTA - What reader should do]
```

### The Listicle Framework
```
[NUMBER] ways to [ACHIEVE OUTCOME] (without [COMMON PAIN])

1. [POINT]
↳ [ELABORATION]

2. [POINT]
↳ [ELABORATION]

...

Which one are you trying first?
```

### The Contrarian Framework
```
Unpopular opinion: [CONTRARIAN TAKE]

Here's why:

[EVIDENCE/STORY]

Most people think [COMMON BELIEF].
But the best [ROLE]s know [CONTRARIAN TRUTH].

[SUPPORTING POINT]

Agree or disagree?
```

### The Before/After Framework
```
[CLIENT TYPE] came to us doing [BEFORE STATE].

They were:
- [PAIN 1]
- [PAIN 2]
- [PAIN 3]

[TIME PERIOD] later:
- [RESULT 1]
- [RESULT 2]
- [RESULT 3]

The shift? [ONE KEY INSIGHT]

Want the same? [CTA]
```

## Part 4: Writing Guidelines

### Voice & Tone
- Conversational, not corporate
- Confident, not arrogant
- Specific, not vague
- Active voice
- Short sentences
- Line breaks for readability

### LinkedIn-Specific Rules
- Hook in first 2 lines (before "see more")
- One idea per post
- 150-300 words optimal
- Use line breaks liberally
- Emojis: sparingly, not excessively
- Hashtags: 3-5 max, at end

### Words to Use
- "Here's what I learned..."
- "Most people don't know..."
- "The truth is..."
- "Here's exactly how..."
- Specific numbers and timeframes

### Words to Avoid
- "Just" (minimizing)
- "Very" / "Really" (weak intensifiers)
- "I think" (be direct)
- "Synergy" / "Leverage" (corporate speak)
- "Revolutionary" / "Game-changing" (hype)

## Part 5: Content Types

### LinkedIn Posts
- **Frequency**: Daily (5x/week minimum)
- **Length**: 150-300 words
- **Images**: Optional but boost engagement
- **Best times**: 8-10 AM, 12-1 PM in audience timezone

### Case Studies
- **Frequency**: 1-2 per month
- **Length**: 500-1000 words
- **Format**: Problem → Solution → Results
- **Required**: Specific metrics, client quote (if permitted)

### Lead Magnets
- **Types**: Checklist, template, guide, calculator
- **Length**: Consumable in <10 minutes
- **Gate**: Email capture
- **Follow-up**: 3-email nurture sequence

## Part 7: Patterns & Learnings

### What Works on LinkedIn (2024-2026)
- Personal stories with business lessons
- Specific numbers and results
- Contrarian takes on common beliefs
- Behind-the-scenes transparency
- Interactive posts (questions, polls)
- Carousels with tactical content

### What Doesn't Work
- Pure self-promotion
- Generic motivational content
- Overly long posts
- Heavy jargon
- Posting without engaging with others
- Inconsistent posting

### Engagement Tactics
- Reply to every comment within 2 hours
- Ask follow-up questions
- Comment on 10 posts before posting yours
- Tag people when relevant (sparingly)
- Share others' wins

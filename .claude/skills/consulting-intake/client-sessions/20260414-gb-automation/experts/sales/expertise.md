# Sales Expertise

## Part 1: Domain Fundamentals

### What is Consultative Selling?
A sales approach focused on understanding the prospect's situation, challenges, and goals before presenting solutions. The goal is to be a trusted advisor, not a pushy salesperson.

### Core Principles
1. **Diagnose before prescribing** - Understand deeply before offering solutions
2. **Sell the outcome, not the service** - Focus on results they'll achieve
3. **Qualify ruthlessly** - Better to lose fast than waste time on bad fits
4. **Create urgency through insight** - Help them see the cost of inaction
5. **Make buying easy** - Remove friction from the decision process

## Part 3: Sales Process

### Stage 1: Qualification (Pre-call)
**Goal**: Confirm fit before investing time
**Actions**:
- Review prospect's LinkedIn/company
- Identify potential pain points
- Prepare 3 personalized questions
- Set call expectations

### Stage 2: Discovery (Call - 15-30 min)
**Goal**: Understand situation, pain, and stakes
**Actions**:
- Build rapport (2 min)
- Ask about current situation (5 min)
- Dig into pain points (10 min)
- Understand stakes and timeline (5 min)
- Qualify budget and authority (5 min)

### Stage 3: Proposal (Within 24 hours)
**Goal**: Present tailored solution with clear ROI
**Actions**:
- Draft proposal based on discovery
- Include specific use cases
- Calculate ROI estimate
- Set clear next steps

### Stage 4: Negotiation (If needed)
**Goal**: Address concerns, find agreement
**Actions**:
- Handle objections
- Adjust scope if needed (not price)
- Create urgency without pressure

### Stage 5: Close
**Goal**: Get signature, start onboarding
**Actions**:
- Send contract
- Process payment
- Schedule intake call
- Hand off to delivery

## Part 4: Discovery Framework

### SPIN Methodology
- **S**ituation - What's the current state?
- **P**roblem - What challenges exist?
- **I**mplication - What happens if unchanged?
- **N**eed-Payoff - What would solving this enable?

### Key Discovery Questions
1. "Walk me through a typical day - where does your time go?"
2. "What's the most frustrating part of your operations?"
3. "If you could clone yourself, what would the clone do?"
4. "What happens when you're sick or on vacation?"
5. "How much is this costing you in time/money/stress?"
6. "What have you tried to solve this?"
7. "What would it mean to have this handled?"
8. "When do you need this solved by?"
9. "Is there a budget allocated for this?"
10. "Who else needs to weigh in on this decision?"

## Part 5: Objection Handling

### Price Objection
**"It's too expensive"**
- "What are you comparing it to?"
- "Let's look at the ROI - if you save 15 hours/week at your rate, that's ${X}/month in saved time alone."
- "What would you expect to pay for an operations hire?"

### Timing Objection
**"Not the right time"**
- "When would be the right time?"
- "What would need to change?"
- "What's the cost of waiting another 3 months?"

### Trust Objection
**"I've been burned before"**
- "Tell me about that experience - what went wrong?"
- "Here's how we're different: {specific contrast}"
- "Would a smaller pilot help you feel confident?"

### Need More Info
**"Send me more information"**
- "Happy to - what specifically would be helpful?"
- "Rather than overload you with docs, can we do a quick 10-minute call to address your questions directly?"

### Need to Think
**"I need to think about it"**
- "Of course - what specifically do you want to think through?"
- "What questions can I answer to help with that decision?"
- "When should I follow up?"

## Part 6: Proposal Framework

### Structure
1. **Executive Summary** - Their situation and our recommendation
2. **Understanding** - Proof we listened during discovery
3. **Approach** - What we'll do (high-level)
4. **Outcomes** - What they'll achieve
5. **Investment** - Pricing and timeline
6. **Next Steps** - Exactly how to proceed

### ROI Calculation
```
Hours saved per week: {X}
Hourly value of prospect's time: ${Y}
Monthly value of time saved: X * Y * 4.3 = ${Z}
Investment: $3,500/month
ROI: (Z - 3500) / 3500 * 100 = {N}%
```

## Part 7: Patterns & Learnings

### Buying Signals
- Asking about implementation details
- Asking about timeline
- Asking about onboarding
- Mentioning other stakeholders
- Forward-looking language ("When we...")

### Warning Signs
- Asking only about price
- Vague about timeline
- Can't articulate the pain
- Won't commit to next steps
- "Just looking" energy

### What Closes Deals
- Clear, specific pain identified
- Quantified cost of inaction
- Decision maker on the call
- Budget confirmed
- Timeline pressure
- Trust established through content

### What Kills Deals
- Vague proposals
- Slow follow-up
- Price before value
- Skipping discovery
- Pushing too hard

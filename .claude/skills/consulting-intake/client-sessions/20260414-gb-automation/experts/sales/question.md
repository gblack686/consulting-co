# Sales Questions

## Pre-Call Research Questions

### To Answer Before Discovery
1. What does their company do?
2. What's their role and likely responsibilities?
3. What content have they engaged with?
4. What pain points can I infer?
5. What mutual connections exist?

## Discovery Call Questions

### Opening (Build Rapport)
1. "Thanks for taking the time - before we dive in, how's your day going?"
2. "I did some homework on {company} - {specific observation}. Did I get that right?"
3. "What prompted you to take this call?"

### Situation (Understand Current State)
4. "Walk me through your current operations - what does a typical week look like?"
5. "What tools and systems are you using right now?"
6. "How big is your team, and who handles what?"

### Problem (Identify Pain)
7. "What's the most frustrating part of your day-to-day?"
8. "Where do you feel like you're spinning your wheels?"
9. "What would your team say is the biggest bottleneck?"
10. "If you could wave a magic wand and fix one thing, what would it be?"

### Implication (Quantify Impact)
11. "How many hours per week would you say this costs you?"
12. "What's the impact on your revenue/clients/team when this doesn't get done?"
13. "What happens when you're out - does everything stop?"
14. "What would it mean for the business to have this solved?"

### Need-Payoff (Visualize Solution)
15. "If we could automate {pain point}, what would you do with that time back?"
16. "What would success look like 90 days from now?"
17. "What would make this investment a no-brainer?"

### Qualification (Confirm Fit)
18. "When are you looking to have this solved by?"
19. "Is there a budget allocated for operations improvements?"
20. "Who else needs to be involved in this decision?"
21. "Have you tried to solve this before? What happened?"

## Proposal Presentation Questions

### Opening
- "Before I walk through the proposal, any questions from our last conversation?"
- "Has anything changed since we last spoke?"

### After Presenting
- "What stands out to you about this approach?"
- "Does this address the pain points we discussed?"
- "What questions do you have?"

### Commitment
- "How does this compare to what you were expecting?"
- "What would need to be true for you to move forward?"
- "If we can address {concern}, are you ready to proceed?"

## Objection Questions

### Uncover Real Objection
- "Help me understand - what's holding you back?"
- "Is it the approach, the timing, or something else?"
- "What would you need to see to feel confident?"

### Price Objection
- "What were you expecting to invest?"
- "Compared to hiring someone, how does this look?"
- "If we can show 3x ROI, would the investment make sense?"

### Timing Objection
- "What's driving the timeline?"
- "What's the cost of waiting another month?"
- "If we could start small and scale, would that help?"

## Follow-Up Questions

### After No Response
- "Just checking in - did you have any questions about the proposal?"
- "Is this still a priority, or has something changed?"
- "Would it help to adjust the scope or timing?"

### After Objection
- "I wanted to follow up on {concern} - have you had time to think it through?"
- "We've since worked with a similar company - would a case study help?"

### After "Let me think"
- "Totally understand - what specifically do you want to think through?"
- "What would help you make this decision?"
- "When should I follow up?"

## Closing Questions

### Trial Close
- "Based on everything we've discussed, how are you feeling about moving forward?"
- "If we addressed {concern}, would you be ready to proceed?"
- "What's your gut telling you?"

### Direct Close
- "Are you ready to get started?"
- "Should I send over the contract?"
- "Want to lock in the start date?"

### Alternative Close
- "Would you like to start with the standard tier or professional?"
- "Would you prefer to start this month or next?"

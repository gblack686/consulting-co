# Session Agenda: OpenClaw Agent Setup

**Duration**: 90 minutes
**Format**: Video call (recorded with permission)

---

## Phase 1: Identity & Soul (15 min)

*We'll define who your agent is and how it should behave.*

Topics covered:
- Your name, timezone, and working hours
- What "done well" looks like for you (quality bar)
- How your agent should handle uncertainty
- Communication style and vibe
- Naming your agent
- Your mission statement and 90-day goals

**You'll walk away with**: Your agent's personality, values, and mission defined.

---

## Phase 2: Tools & Infrastructure (15 min)

*We'll inventory your tech stack and set up the foundation.*

Topics covered:
- What device your agent will run on
- Your daily apps and tools (with API availability)
- Existing API keys and subscriptions
- Communication channel (WhatsApp, Telegram, Discord)
- Cost vs. quality preferences for AI models

**You'll walk away with**: A complete tool inventory and infrastructure plan.

---

## Phase 3: Domain Discovery & Workflows (30 min)

*The core of the session — we'll map out your workflows in detail.*

Topics covered:
- Your 3-5 "departments" (domains)
- For each domain's top workflow:
  - The most annoying recurring task
  - Step-by-step walkthrough ("train a new hire")
  - What triggers it (time, event, decision)
  - What the output looks like
  - Which decisions are yours vs. mechanical
  - What happens if it goes wrong
- Periodic check-in tasks (heartbeat)
- Your ideal morning briefing

**You'll walk away with**: Detailed workflow maps for your top domains.

---

## Phase 4: Autonomy & Safety (10 min)

*We'll set boundaries for what your agent can and can't do alone.*

Topics covered:
- Your autonomy comfort level (ask everything ↔ just get it done)
- External communication permissions (emails, tweets, texts)
- Who's allowed to talk to your agent
- Session memory preferences (remember forever vs. reset daily)

**You'll walk away with**: Clear autonomy rules and safety boundaries.

---

## Phase 5: Synthesis & Mission Statement (10 min)

*We'll read back everything and make sure it feels right.*

Topics covered:
- Draft mission statement review
- Final adjustments
- Delivery timeline and next steps

**You'll walk away with**: A finalized mission statement and clear picture of what's being built.

---

## Buffer (10 min)

Reserved for:
- Follow-up questions
- Additional workflow detail
- Anything we didn't get to

---

## After the Session

| Timeline | What Happens |
|----------|-------------|
| Day 1 | We process your transcript and build your workspace files |
| Days 2-3 | We build domain expert systems (one per department) |
| Day 3-4 | Quality validation and testing |
| Day 4-5 | Deployment to your OpenClaw instance |
| Day 5 | Walkthrough call: we show you how everything works |

You'll receive:
- All workspace files (SOUL.md, USER.md, etc.)
- Expert systems per domain (self-improving over time)
- Deployed skills with cron jobs running
- A quality report with scores for every component
- Documentation for ongoing self-improvement

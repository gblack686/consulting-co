**Talk through these questions (aim for 10–20 minutes total):**

1. **Your tools** — Walk through every app or tool you use at least weekly. What do you use it for? (e.g. "I use Notion for project management, Gmail for client email, Stripe for payments…")

2. **Your departments** — If you hired 3–5 people to replace you, what would each person own? Give each "role" a name. (e.g. "Content, Business Development, Client Ops, Personal Admin")

3. **Your most annoying task** — Describe one thing you do at least weekly that takes 30+ minutes and follows the same steps every time. Walk through exactly what you do.

4. **Your perfect morning briefing** — What would you want a message from your AI agent to tell you every morning? Be specific — what data, what format, what tone?

5. **Your agent's personality** — What should your agent call you? What vibe do you want — professional, casual, sharp, playful? Got a name in mind?

No need to be polished. Stream of consciousness is perfect. The transcript captures everything.
